Vehicle cutouts

Six reference photos were edited using the built-in image editing tool to remove their backgrounds. Raize already had a transparent background. Original photos are preserved. The 2021 and 2022 Tucson retain their shared reference image.

Editing prompt: Remove the entire background, road, showroom, buildings, other vehicles and signage. Keep the same main vehicle, body shape, paint color, wheels, front details and camera angle. Produce a photorealistic vehicle cutout with transparent alpha, no floor or added text. Center the complete vehicle with all wheels visible and transparent padding.
